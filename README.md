AIM:

ㅤ
	To sketch the given drawing with dimensions using fusion 360 tool.
  
REQUIREMENTS:

1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3. 16GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 2 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload
   
PROCEDURE:

1. First, click on Create Sketch and select the Top Plane.
   
<img width="258" height="271" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-05 204031" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/418dea51-9b3f-49aa-9ba1-a3252f01a8f0" />

2. Construct the U-shaped 2D profile with a length of 52 mm, thickness of 11 mm and a gap of 18 mm.
   
<img width="528" height="448" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-05 211448" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3499acbe-aa74-4fb2-8d42-f61b4c310eff" />

3. Click on Finish Sketch and extrude the profile to a height of 32 mm.
   
<img width="742" height="663" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-05 211636" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a339fbb9-b909-42a6-849a-227544e4f60b" />

4. Select the Fillet option, use Shift + Click to select the four required edges and apply a fillet radius of 16 mm to create the rounded front portion.
   
<img width="645" height="585" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-05 211711" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/15242750-1cf8-4bb1-a993-7918244a9471" />

5. Select the centre of the rounded portion and create a hole with a diameter of 16 mm. Finish the sketch and use the Extrude Cut option to create the hole through the body.
   
<img width="675" height="612" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-05 211756" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3b3ddc10-d862-4eea-8fb5-f62be21ddbcb" />

6. Next, select the end face of the extended portion and create a new sketch on it.
   
7. Draw a circle with a diameter of 18 mm at the centre of the front face.
   
<img width="510" height="415" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-05 212036" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1d7480c5-8a71-4de5-9a8f-8f03a2f4f7a9" />

8. Click on Finish Sketch and extrude the circle to a length of 32 mm.
   
9. In the Extrude settings, select the Join option instead of Cut to connect the cylindrical portion to the main body.
